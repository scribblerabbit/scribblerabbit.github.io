I’m Michelle Parwoto Wirono (a.k.a. Scribble Rabbit), an Indonesian-born illustrator and visual development artist based in Los Angeles, CA. Growing up, movies like Mulan inspired me to bring more Asian and Chinese-Indonesian voices into animation. I moved to the U.S. in 2018 to study at Columbus College of Art & Design, where I graduated as valedictorian with a BFA in Illustration and a minor in 2D Animation. Outside of work, I love cafe hopping, thrifting, and daydreaming about opening a cat café.
