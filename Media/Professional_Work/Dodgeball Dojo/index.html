Dodgeball Dojo

Dodgeball Dojo (2025) is a mobile card game inspired by Big 2, the classic family card game enjoyed by millions worldwide. Reimagined with dynamic, manga-inspired animations, the game brings a fresh, modern energy to familiar gameplay. As one of the main concept artists at FUN-GI Games, I helped shape the overall visual direction and art style. I took ownership of character, creature, and icon designs for a new IP, collaborating closely with cross-disciplinary teams to align art with gameplay and narrative goals.

Art Direction by Mia Bai & Jesse Maccabe
