House Flip

House Flip (2018) is a cozy mobile game where players renovate and sell homes as realtors. At FUN-GI Games, I served as one of the main concept artists, designing characters, in-game graphics, UI, and marketing visuals that expanded the world of this beloved IP. I collaborated closely with designers and writers to keep the visual direction consistent with the game’s narrative and player experience.

Art Direction by Mia Bai & Jesse Maccabe
